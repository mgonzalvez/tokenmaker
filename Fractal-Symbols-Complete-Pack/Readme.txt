Fractal Symbols: Board Game Library by Felix Thålin (fractalsymbols.com)

Fractal Symbols is an easy-to-use library of symbols for prototyping and publishing board games.

The library is divided into sections, with symbols grouped based on use.
For example, all card symbols go into the Card section, all symbols related to Combat are stored in the Combat section.
Each section is showcased below with its title, a sample of symbols, and a rough description of what’s in that section.

Technical information
--------------------
The symbols are available in two formats: SVG and PNG (rendered at 1000x1000 resolution).

Disclaimer
----------
Some symbols are taken from public domain sites and are usually modified.
I have chosen to include them in the library to make them easier to find and use.

However due to the nature of public domain sites where anyone can upload anything and claim
they have the right to publish it to public domain, some symbols may have been stolen.

If you see the work of your own or someone else inside this library and feel it has been used
without proper permission or attribution, please contact me and I’ll look into solving that issue.
